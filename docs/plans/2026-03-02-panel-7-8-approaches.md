# Panel 7 & 8 Design Approaches — CWIEME Berlin 2026

Date: 2026-03-02
Status: Option A selected for both panels. Others saved for future reference.

## Panel 7 — Credentials Wall ("Why Universal Metals")

### A: "Editorial Spread" [SELECTED]
Magazine-editorial layout. Each credential gets a dramatic typographic section.
- No cards — credentials flow as full-width "chapters"
- Key stats rendered at massive scale (400px+) ARE the visual element
- Ghost watermark numbers shift to left margin like page numbers
- 2-3 credential blocks separated by thin copper rules
- Consider merging "2x CCR Lines" + "5 Enamelling Lines" into "Manufacturing Capacity"

### B: "Data Monument"
Full-bleed infographic style — data viz IS the art.
- Expand sustainability rings to 700-800px diameter
- Each credential gets a unique data-viz treatment (arc gauges, giant numerals, bars)
- Annual-report aesthetic
- Risk: could look cluttered or poster-like

### C: "Statement Wall"
Pure typography — massive text, no containers.
- Claims stack vertically in raw bold type
- Text IS the design — no cards, borders, or icons
- Jenny Holzer / Barbara Kruger art-installation feel
- Risk: could feel like shouting if not balanced

## Panel 8 — Contact CTA ("Talk to Us")

### A: "Warm Copper Close" [SELECTED]
Subtle warm gradient + brushed-metal texture for premium feel.
- Background: vertical gradient white (top) to warm cream/copper wash (bottom)
- Brushed-metal texture overlay at 5-8% opacity
- "Talk to Us" with subtle copper text-shadow
- QR cards with frosted-glass treatment
- URL sits on warmest part of gradient

### B: "Premium Brand Card"
Entire panel treated as a luxury oversized business card.
- Full cream/champagne background
- Copper "foil-stamped" accents (gradient borders simulating metallic foil)
- Thin copper border around entire content area
- Risk: could feel stiff if overdone

### C: "The Bookend"
Mirror the left wall's visual weight for symmetry.
- Large copper accent band behind the CTA
- Creates visual bookends when viewing the full wall
- Depends on Panel 1/2 redesign outcome
