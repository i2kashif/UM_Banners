Est. 1994 · Lahore, Pakistan

Our Journey

1994
Unit 1 Photo

Founded Universal Metals in Lahore. Began production of enamelled copper wire at Unit 1.

2000
Unit 2 Photo

Established Unit 2 for overhead conductor manufacturing, entering the power transmission sector.

2006
CCR Line Photo

Installed Copper CCR line at Unit 3, bringing rod production in-house for the first time.

2013
Factory Photo

Added new enamelling capacity and launched paper covered copper strip production at Unit 3.

2016
Al CCR Photo

Installed Aluminium CCR line at Unit 1, extending in-house rod production to aluminium grade.

2020
New Hall Photo

Commissioned aluminium enamelling machines and second European-technology Aluminium CCR line at Unit 3.

2023
Enamelling Photo

Installed new-generation enamelling machines and submersible winding wire production line.

2025
Unit 4 Photo

Completed Unit 4 — new cable factory. Launched insulation materials production. Four manufacturing units operational.
