Universal Metals Pakistan Limited

Est. 1994 · Lahore, Pakistan

30 Years of Growth. From Rod to Wire.

1994

Founded Universal Metals in Lahore. Began production of enamelled copper wire at Unit 1.

Founding Year
2000

Expanded into power cables & conductors — established Unit 2 for overhead conductor manufacturing.

2006

Installed Copper CCR line at Unit 3, bringing rod production in-house for the first time.

Vertical Integration
2013

Added new enamelling hall capacity and launched paper covered copper strip production at Unit 3.

2016

Installed Aluminium CCR line at Unit 1 — extending in-house rod production to aluminium grade.

Aluminium Expansion
2020

Commissioned aluminium enamelling machines and a second European-technology Aluminium CCR line at Unit 3.

2023

Installed new-generation enamelling machines and submersible winding wire production line.

2025

Completed Unit 4 — new cable factory. Launched insulation materials production. Four full manufacturing units operational.

Latest Milestone

Three decades of manufacturing excellence — all under one roof in Lahore.
